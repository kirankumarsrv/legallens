--- STATEMENT BEGIN ---

My name is Arun Kumar Singh, 38 years old. I own Kiosk 15 in the Electronics 
Section of Central Shopping Mall, Bangalore. 

On January 15, 2025, I was managing my store as usual. The mall was moderately 
busy with regular shopping traffic. At approximately 10:30 PM, I noticed a man 
(later identified as Munjappa) entering the electronics section. He appeared to 
be browsing initially, but his behavior soon became suspicious.

Around 10:35 PM, I was arranging merchandise near the back of my kiosk when I 
noticed that several high-value items were missing from my display. Specifically:
- 1 Laptop (Worth Rs. 1,25,000)
- 2 Mobile phones from the premium display (Worth approximately Rs. 30,000)

I immediately called out to the person, but he started moving toward the exit. 
At that exact moment, I saw Police Officer Arun Kumar (Senior Constable) on patrol 
duty near the electronics section. I immediately flagged him down and reported the 
suspicious person and the missing items.

Officer Arun Kumar quickly responded and apprehended the accused near the mall exit. 
Upon search, the stolen items were found in the accused's possession. 

The suspect did not cooperate during the apprehension process and attempted to 
flee, but was successfully arrested within minutes.

I am confident that Munjappa is the person who stole items from my store, and 
I am willing to testify in court.

--- STATEMENT END ---
