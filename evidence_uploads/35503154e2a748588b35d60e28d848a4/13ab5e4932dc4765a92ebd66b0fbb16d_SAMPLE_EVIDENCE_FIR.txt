INCIDENT DESCRIPTION
===================================================================

On the date and time mentioned above, the complainant Amit Sharma was riding his two-wheeler 
(Hero MotoCorp, Registration: DL-02-CD-9012) lawfully across the intersection of Ring Road 
and Sector 12. The traffic signal was in the complainant's favor (Green light for the direction 
from which he was approaching).

At the same time, the accused Rajesh Kumar was operating a commercial taxi in a reckless 
and negligent manner. The vehicle was traveling at a speed significantly exceeding the 
prescribed speed limit for urban roads (estimated 70-80 km/h in a 40 km/h zone).

The accused's vehicle failed to stop at the red traffic signal and proceeded to cross the 
intersection at high speed. As a result, the vehicle collided with the complainant's two-wheeler, 
causing the complainant to be thrown from the vehicle onto the road.
