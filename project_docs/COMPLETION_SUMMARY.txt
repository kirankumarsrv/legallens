## What Was Accomplished

### 🐛 The Bug That Was Fixed
**Problem:** Facts were being gathered twice
- Phase 1: Retrieved 6 statute facts
- Human approval: Approved 6 facts ✓
- Phase 2: Retrieved 456 facts AGAIN ✗ (Bug!)

**Result:** 462 facts sent to LLM instead of 11 → overwhelming context, poor analysis

### ✨ The Solution Implemented
**Fact Locking Mechanism:**
1. Facts are stored in `FactStorage` with metadata
2. Human approval locks facts (status = "approved_locked")
3. Legal analysis uses locked facts (no re-retrieval)
4. Only NEW precedents retrieved in Phase 2 (5 facts, not duplicates)

**Result:** 11 facts sent to LLM (97.6% reduction) → focused, efficient analysis
