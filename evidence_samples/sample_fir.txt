FACTS OF THE CASE:

1. The complainant, Rajesh Kumar, has been employed with TechCorp India Pvt Ltd as a Senior Software Engineer for the past 8 years with unblemished record and no disciplinary action.

2. During July 2024, the IT department of TechCorp installed surveillance software on all employee systems with the stated purpose of "system security and monitoring unauthorized access."

3. In October 2024, the IT Manager, Mr. Vikram Sharma, casually mentioned to the complainant details about the complainant's personal health condition which was discussed in personal emails between the complainant and his doctor.

4. Upon investigation by the complainant, it was discovered that:
   - A keylogger software was installed on his official workstation
   - The software was logging all keystrokes including personal email passwords
   - Personal email accounts (Gmail, Yahoo) were being monitored
   - Emails to family members, spouse, and doctor were being intercepted and logged
   - Medical records and personal financial information were accessed

5. The accused company did not:
   - Obtain written consent from the employee
   - Disclose the monitoring in any employee handbook or policy
   - Inform the employee of the surveillance
   - Provide any technical justification for monitoring personal email accounts

6. The complainant has received threats of termination if the matter is escalated, constituting further criminal intimidation.

EVIDENCE:
- Email records showing personal communications accessed
- Screenshots of keylogger software logs
- IT Security audit report
- Employee handbook (no mention of personal email monitoring)
- Medical email correspondence
- Witness statements from 3 other employees subjected to similar monitoring
