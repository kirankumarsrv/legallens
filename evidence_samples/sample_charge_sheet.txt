CHARGE SHEET

Police Station: Cyber Crime Cell, New Delhi
Charge Sheet Number: CS-789/2024
Date: November 10, 2024
Investigating Officer: Inspector Amit Singh

CASE DETAILS:
Original FIR: 12345/2024
Petitioner: Rajesh Kumar
Respondent: TechCorp India Pvt Ltd and others

FINDINGS OF INVESTIGATION:

1. UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS CONFIRMED
   - Technical audit by forensic team confirms keylogger installation
   - Software logs 100% of keystrokes including personal passwords
   - Scope: Entire personal email communications (Gmail account since 2015)
   - Duration: Minimum 3 months (July-October 2024), possibly longer

2. NO EMPLOYEE CONSENT
   - Employee handbook reviewed: NO mention of personal email monitoring
   - No consent forms signed by complainant
   - No notification of surveillance to employee
   - Policy violation by company itself (states monitoring only work email)

3. MEDICAL PRIVACY BREACH
   - IT Manager disclosed to others that he "knew" about employee's health condition
   - Evidence: Email chain between employee and cardiologist intercepted
   - Private health information used as basis for mocking remarks
   - Clear violation of medical confidentiality

4. CRIMINAL INTENT ESTABLISHED
   - Installation of spy software indicates deliberate action
   - Accessing personal emails beyond scope of employment
   - Disclosure of private information shows misuse
   - Threats to employee following discovery confirm consciousness of guilt

5. FINANCIAL DATA ACCESSED
   - Banking and investment email communications accessed
   - Credit card information visible in email receipts
   - Potential identity theft risk established

6. CORROBORATING EVIDENCE
   - 3 other employees came forward with similar complaints
   - Identical pattern of monitoring across multiple employees
   - No legitimate business purpose established for personal email access

LEGAL VIOLATIONS ESTABLISHED:

Article 21, Indian Constitution - Right to Privacy (Justice K.S. Puttaswamy Judgment)
- Established privacy as fundamental right
- Monitoring personal communications is violation

IPC Section 354C - Violation of Privacy
- "Watching or engaging in voyeurism" defined broadly
- Electronic surveillance is covered

IPC Section 406 - Criminal Breach of Trust
- Employer entrusted with workplace access
- Misused for personal data collection

IT Act 2000 Section 66 - Unauthorized Access
- Accessing computer systems without authorization
- Criminal offense with imprisonment up to 3 years

IT Act 2000 Section 43 - Damage to computer systems
- Unauthorized access and data interception
- Civil liability + damages

IPC Section 503, 506 - Criminal Intimidation
- Threats of termination if matter escalated
- Clear intimidation following discovery

CONCLUSION:
Evidence conclusively establishes that TechCorp India Pvt Ltd through its officials:
1. Installed unauthorized surveillance software
2. Accessed and monitored personal private communications
3. Breached fundamental right to privacy
4. Committed multiple criminal offenses
5. Intimidated complainant to suppress investigation

RECOMMENDATION:
Charge under IPC Sections 354C, 406, 66 (IT Act) and register FIR for prosecution.
