Case Title: State vs. Rajesh Kumar - Motor Vehicle Act Violation

Problem Statement:
------------------

On December 15, 2024, at approximately 3:45 PM, a motor vehicle accident occurred at the intersection of 
Ring Road and Sector 12, City Central. The defendant, Rajesh Kumar (Age: 35, Resident of Apartment 5B, 
Lakeside Complex), was driving a commercial taxi (License Plate: DL-01-AB-5678) that collided with a 
two-wheeler driven by the complainant, Amit Sharma (Age: 28, Resident of Plot 45, Green Heights).

Sequence of Events:
- The complainant was legally crossing the intersection with a green signal
- The defendant's vehicle approached the intersection at high speed
- The vehicle failed to stop at the red signal and collided with the two-wheeler
- The complainant was thrown from the vehicle and suffered multiple injuries
- Police arrived at the scene within 15 minutes of the accident
- An FIR was filed under sections 279, 304A, and 338 of the Indian Penal Code
- The defendant was detained and breathalyzer test showed blood alcohol content of 0.08%

Key Evidence Available:
- CCTV footage from nearby traffic camera (90 seconds before and after collision)
- Medical report from City Central Hospital documenting injuries
- Traffic police accident report (challan number: TC/DL/2024/45678)
- Witness statements from 3 bystanders
- Vehicle damage assessment report
- Driver's license and vehicle registration documents
- Breathalyzer test results

Questions to Resolve:
1. Was the defendant's vehicle driving at excessive speed?
2. Did the defendant run the red light intentionally or due to mechanical failure?
3. Was the defendant under the influence of alcohol during the accident?
4. What is the extent of liability under the Motor Vehicle Act, 1988?
5. What compensation should be awarded to the victim?
6. Should the defendant's driving license be revoked?

Legal Jurisdiction:
- Court: District Court, Central District
- Applicable Laws: Indian Penal Code (IPC) Sections 279, 304A, 338
              Motor Vehicle Act, 1988 Sections 143, 144, 184
              Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (if applicable)
- Previous Similar Cases: Case references - 2020/DL/5432 (similar motor vehicle negligence)
